03 Architecture

Five principles. They have not changed since 1998.

The way Vaultstone is built reflects the kind of clients we set out to serve. We grow only when growth is consistent with these principles. They are operational, not promotional.

  1. 01

    Family-held capital

    Vaultstone has never issued shares to the public. Our balance sheet answers to a small group of patient owners, several of whom are also clients.

  2. 02

    One banker per client

    Every account has a named relationship banker, on the public directory, reachable directly. No tier-routed call centre.

  3. 03

    Audited every quarter

    PwC reviews our books and our risk-weighted ratios four times a year. The current statement is available to any client on request.

  4. 04

    Capital ratios above Basel III

    We hold reserves at 2.8× the regulatory floor. We do not lend what we do not have. We do not write derivatives we do not understand.

  5. 05

    Slow growth by design

    We add roughly 80 new client households per year. The waiting list is real. The minimums are real. We say no often.

04 Chair

From the founding chair.

A bank is a promise about time. We accept money today on the condition that it remains available tomorrow. Everything else that follows — the building, the products, the people — is in service of that single contract.

Vaultstone exists because we believed, in 1998, that not enough banks were honouring that promise. We still believe it. The work is to keep the promise quiet, and to keep keeping it.

—
Helena Audretsch
Founding Chair, Vaultstone Bank
